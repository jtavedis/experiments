You are a {% if role == 'buyer' %}Buyer{% else %}Seller{% endif %} for all {{ C.NUM_ROUNDS }} trading periods.

We are going to set up a market in which you will be buyers or sellers of home painting services. At the start of every period you receive a new private card. Only you can see your number. Buyers' cards are red and sellers' cards are black.

  • Buyers: your number is the most you are willing to pay. You may buy one unit per period. You earn value − price. For example, with a 9 you buy at $4 and earn $9 − $4 = $5. You may not buy above your value.
  • Sellers: your number is your cost, the lowest price you can accept. You may sell one unit per period. You earn price − cost. For example, with a 2 you sell at $3 and earn $3 − $2 = $1. You may not sell below your cost.
  • If you do not trade, you earn $0 that period.
Trading on screen

Each period lasts {{ C.TRADING_SECONDS }} seconds (5 minutes). Every trader appears as a dot on the trading floor: red dots are buyers and black dots are sellers.

  • Ask around. Click on a dot to see the price that trader is announcing, if any, and to open a private chat with them. You can chat with as many traders on the other side of the market as you like.
  • Announce a price (optional). This is what you would tell anyone who asks. It is not binding.
  • Make an offer. In a chat, type a price and press Offer. If the other trader accepts, the trade is made. You can withdraw an offer while it is still open, and a new offer to the same person replaces your old one.
  • Choose among offers. Offers sent to you appear at the top of your screen. You may accept one, decline any of them, or wait.
  • Front desk. Every accepted trade is checked. A price below the seller's cost or above the buyer's value is invalidated, and both traders go back to negotiating.
  • Whiteboard. Every valid trade price is posted on the whiteboard for everyone to see. The two traders then turn grey and wait for the period to end.

Some sellers with high costs and some buyers with low values may not be able to trade. Do not be discouraged: new cards are dealt every period. All earnings are hypothetical.
