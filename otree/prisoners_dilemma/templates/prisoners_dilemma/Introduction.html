How the game works

You will be matched with a partner and play a card game for {{ C.NUM_ROUNDS }} rounds. Your partner is the same person for all rounds.

Each round you will be shown two cards — a RED card and a BLACK card. Choose one by clicking on it. Both you and your partner choose at the same time, without seeing each other's choice. Once both of you have chosen, the cards are revealed and your earnings for the round are calculated.

🔴 Playing the Red Card
  • Your earnings increase by $2.
  • Your partner's earnings are unchanged by your choice.
⚫ Playing the Black Card
  • Your earnings are unchanged by your choice.
  • Your partner's earnings increase by $3.
Payoff table — Rounds 1–5
Partner plays…
You play… 🔴 Red ⚫ Black
🔴 Red $2 / $2 $5 / $0
⚫ Black $0 / $5 $3 / $3

Format: Your earnings / Partner's earnings

Structure
  • Rounds 1–5 — Payoffs are as described above.
  • Rounds 6–10 — Payoffs will change. You will see the new table before Round 6.
  • Your total earnings equal the sum across all 10 rounds.
  • All earnings are hypothetical.

Please do not discuss your choice with your partner until after you have both submitted your card.
