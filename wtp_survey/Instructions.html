Overview

In this exercise, you'll be asked to make choices that reflect your preferences between receiving a product or receiving money.

To simulate a real business setting, we are using a mechanism commonly employed in focus groups and pricing studies.

Participation Fee

Each participant receives a hypothetical €5 participation fee. You won't see this in your payoff, but you should treat it as part of the scenario.

Prize Draw

At the end of the study, one participant will be randomly selected as the winner of a product offer.

For the winner:

  • One product is randomly selected.
  • A price is randomly drawn for that product.
  • If the price is less than or equal to your stated willingness to pay (WTP), you will receive the product.
  • If the price is higher than your WTP, you will receive nothing.

Everyone else receives only the hypothetical €5 participation fee.

How to State Your Preferences

For each product, you'll see a list of choices between:

  • The product, and
  • A cash amount (e.g., "Product or €10")

For each row, choose the option you prefer. You may switch from choosing the product to choosing the cash amount as prices increase, but you should switch at most once.

On the first row where you choose the cash, a dropdown will appear allowing you to specify a more precise value where you are indifferent between the two options.

Your Goal

Please answer honestly — as if the outcome will affect what you receive — because for one randomly chosen participant, it will.

This exercise simulates how companies elicit willingness to pay for pricing decisions. Your choices help estimate how much value customers place on different products.
