How the game works

You and everyone else in the room play together for {{ C.NUM_ROUNDS }} rounds. Each round you hold 4 cards: two RED and two BLACK.

Each round you play two of your four cards onto a common stack and keep the other two. All choices are anonymous — no one learns who played which cards.

How earnings are calculated

Your earnings each round come from your red cards:

Earnings = ${{ dollars_per_red_kept }} × (red cards you keep)  +  $1 × (total red cards in the whole stack)
  • Each red card you keep earns you ${{ dollars_per_red_kept }}.
  • Black cards you keep earn you nothing.
  • Every red card placed on the stack (by anyone) adds $1 to everyone's earnings.
  • Black cards on the stack have no effect.
Structure
  • Rounds 1–4 — Standard game, as described above.
  • Rounds 5–8 — The structure changes: a risk of losing the round's earnings is introduced.
  • Rounds 9–12 — The structure changes again, with a higher risk.

You will see a full explanation of each new structure before it begins.

All earnings are hypothetical.
