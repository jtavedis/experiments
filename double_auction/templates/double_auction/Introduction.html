Overview

You are about to participate in a market for home painting services. You will be randomly assigned the role of either a Buyer or a Seller, and you will keep that role for all {{ C.NUM_ROUNDS }} trading periods.

At the start of each period, every player receives a private number — your value (if you are a buyer) or your cost (if you are a seller). These numbers are reshuffled at the start of every new period.

🛒 Buyers
  • Your value is the maximum price you are willing to pay.
  • You earn value − price on each successful trade.
  • You cannot accept a price above your value.
  • If you do not trade, your earnings for the period are $0.
Example: Value = $9, Trade price = $4 → Earnings = $9 − $4 = $5
🏠 Sellers
  • Your cost is the minimum price you are willing to accept.
  • You earn price − cost on each successful trade.
  • You cannot accept a price below your cost.
  • If you do not trade, your earnings for the period are $0.
Example: Cost = $2, Trade price = $3 → Earnings = $3 − $2 = $1
How trading works
  1. You will see a Market Board showing all active bids (from buyers) and asks (from sellers).
  2. Post your own bid or ask by entering a price and clicking Post.
  3. Accept any standing offer from the other side by clicking Accept.
  4. You may update or retract your standing offer at any time before it is accepted.
  5. Each player may complete at most one trade per period.
  6. The trading period lasts {{ C.TRADING_SECONDS }} seconds. All completed trade prices are publicly announced on the board.

Your earnings are hypothetical. Please do not discuss your private value or cost with other participants.
